WHY BIBLEERA EXPLORER?

Learning shouldn't feel overwhelming.

BibleEra Explorer is a student capstone project designed to help beginners explore major events in the Bible. Instead of showing long pages of text, it gives you a simple timeline with short summaries and passages you can look up yourself.

The timeline is a learning guide, not a complete history or replacement for reading Scripture. More events and features will be added as the project develops.
